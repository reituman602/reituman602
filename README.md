## 🚀 About Me

I've spent years in the trenches of cross-border e-commerce — from manually building WordPress + WooCommerce stores, to architecting multi-currency payment flows with Stripe, Airwallex, and Payoneer, to running profitable Facebook and TikTok ad campaigns.

In 2026, I launched **[KuajingBase (跨境基地)](https://kuajingbase.com)** — a no-BS guide site for Chinese cross-border sellers who want to build real, sustainable businesses. Everything on the site comes from **firsthand testing and real spending**, not regurgitated advice.

- 🏗️ **Store Architecture**: WooCommerce, Shopify, Elementor, custom themes
- 💳 **Payment Systems**: Stripe, Airwallex, WorldFirst, Payoneer, Wise, PayPal
- 🖥️ **Hosting & VPS**: Cloudways, DigitalOcean, Vultr, Linux LNMP stacks
- 📈 **Traffic & SEO**: Google organic, TikTok Ads, Facebook Ads, content SEO
- 🤖 **AI Tooling**: Integrating AI into product selection, copywriting, and operations
